## Strategic framing

Rora operates in two distinct search markets simultaneously:

1. **Local intent** — small business owners in the Fylde Coast / Lancashire searching for someone to help with AI or automation. Low volume, high conversion. **Win these first.**

2. **UK-wide niche intent** — people searching for Simpro automation, trades-specific AI, or specific workflow problems. Low competition because it's specific. **Medium-term opportunity.**

3. **Category intent** — broad terms like "AI for small business" or "business automation consultant". High volume, high competition, dominated by national agencies. **Ignore for now — build topical authority first.**
