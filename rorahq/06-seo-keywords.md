# Rora — SEO Keyword Research
**File:** `06-seo-keywords.md`
**Status:** Production-ready — use for content planning and page targeting
**Scope:** UK SMB/trades owners searching for AI automation. Blackpool/Lancashire primary. UK-wide secondary.
**Method:** Intent-based clustering. Difficulty ratings based on competition reasoning, not a tool pull.

---

## How to Read This Document

Each cluster maps to a specific part of the site. Prioritise the keywords marked **immediate** — they are low-competition, high-intent, and Rora already has the page to target them. Keywords marked **3 months** need content that doesn't fully exist yet. Keywords marked **6 months** are competitive terms worth building toward.

## TOP 3 QUICK WINS

These are the three highest-priority keywords based on a combination of: low difficulty, high commercial intent, and a direct match to an existing Rora page.

### Quick Win 1: "AI consultant Blackpool"
- **Why:** Near-zero competition. Rora is the only business doing this in Blackpool. Anyone searching this is a qualified buyer.
- **Page:** `/about`
- **Fix needed:** Add "AI consultant Blackpool" to the /about page H1 or opening paragraph. Add it to the title tag. Get Google Business Profile verified.
- **Time to rank:** 2–4 weeks after GBP verification.

### Quick Win 2: "AI automation consultant UK"
- **Why:** Clear transactional intent, growing search trend, and the /what-we-build page is the natural landing point. Competition is mainly generic AI platforms, not boutique consultants.
- **Page:** `/what-we-build`
- **Fix needed:** Add the phrase to the page H1 and meta description. Include it once in the first 100 words of body copy.
- **Time to rank:** 4–8 weeks.

### Quick Win 3: "Simpro automation consultant" / "n8n consultant UK"
- **Why:** Hyper-specific, near-zero competition, and the person searching this is already sold on the concept — they just need a consultant. One mention on the services page is enough to rank.
- **Page:** `/what-we-build`
- **Fix needed:** Add a "Tools we work with" section mentioning Simpro, n8n, Make.com, Zapier, Xero, Google Workspace. Two sentences per tool is enough.
- **Time to rank:** 2–6 weeks.
