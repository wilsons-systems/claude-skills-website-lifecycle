## Keyword Gaps by Cluster

### Cluster 1 — EV Charger Installation (No Page Exists)

**Priority: Immediate. Create `/ev-charger-installation/`.**

EV charger installation is a fast-growing search category. The market for domestic EV charger installation in the UK has been growing at 35–40% year-on-year as EV adoption accelerates. Local competition for this keyword on the Fylde Coast is currently low. Wilsons already does this work — there is no reason not to have a page.

**Target keywords:**
- "EV charger installation Lytham St Annes" — highest value (affluent audience, new EVs common)
- "EV charger installation Blackpool"
- "electric car charger installer Fylde Coast"
- "EV charger installer Lancashire"
- "home EV charger installation NICEIC"
- "Pod Point installer Blackpool" / "Zappi charger installer Lancashire" (brand-specific searches)
- "OZEV approved installer Lancashire" (grant-related search — if Wilsons is OZEV registered)

**Commercial intent signals:** These are people ready to book. Search volume is growing and competition in the local area has not caught up.

**Page target:** H1 "EV Charger Installation on the Fylde Coast — NICEIC Certified". See `05-seo-onpage.md` for full spec.

### Cluster 3 — Lytham Premium Services (Beyond Electrician)

**Priority: 3–6 months. New location-specific pages for non-electrical services.**

Currently "electrician lytham st annes" is well covered. But the affluent Lytham audience searching for smart home installers or CCTV in their area has no Lytham-specific landing page — they land on generic service pages.

**Target keywords (currently unowned by any specific page):**
- "smart home installer Lytham St Annes" — high value, zero local competition
- "CCTV installation Lytham St Annes" — high-net-worth homeowners are a security-conscious audience
- "home cinema Lytham St Annes" — premium AV for large homes
- "Control4 installer Lytham St Annes" — brand-specific, captures the committed buyer
- "security alarm Lytham St Annes" — Texecom for high-end residential

**Proposed new pages:**
- `/smart-homes-lytham-st-annes/` — captures smart home + Control4 + AV searches for the Lytham audience
- `/cctv-lytham-st-annes/` — captures CCTV + security alarm searches for the Lytham audience

These are not urgent — the existing service pages capture some of this traffic. But they would make the Lytham targeting more precise and create additional ranking opportunities for the premium audience.

### Cluster 4 — Blog / Guides (Informational Traffic)

**Priority: Ongoing. 1–2 new guides per month under `/guides/`.**

Informational searches capture buyers at the research stage — before they are ready to call. A homeowner searching "how much does a house rewire cost" is planning a rewire. Getting in front of them at that stage, and being the business that answered their question clearly, creates recall when they are ready to book.

**Target keyword opportunities:**

| Keyword | Est. intent | Aligns to | Existing guide? |
|---|---|---|---|
| "how much does a house rewire cost UK" | Informational / planning | /house-rewiring/ | No — create |
| "EICR landlord certificate cost" | Commercial / landlords | /eicr-testing/ | No — create |
| "Control4 vs Lutron" | Buyer research | Smart homes page | Partial (control4-vs-diy exists) |
| "EV charger cost UK" | Informational / planning | /ev-charger-installation/ | No — create with page |
| "home cinema cost UK" | Buyer research / premium | Home cinema page | No — create |
| "what is a consumer unit" | Informational / awareness | /consumer-unit-upgrades/ | No — could add |
| "Texecom Connect app review" | Informational / product | Security alarms page | Yes — guide exists |
| "DIY vs professional alarm" | Informational / decision | Security alarms page | Yes — guide exists |

**Content approach:** Follow the existing guide format — long-form, detailed, genuinely useful. Attribute to Ryan Wilson. Include a clear CTA back to the relevant service page at the end.

### Cluster 5 — Commercial (Currently Thin)

**Priority: 6 months. Potential hub page at `/commercial/`.**

Commercial work is mentioned on existing service pages but there is no dedicated commercial landing page. The commercial keyword cluster is less competitive than residential in this area and has strong intent — a facilities manager searching for a commercial electrician is a high-value lead.

**Target keywords:**
- "commercial electrician Blackpool"
- "commercial CCTV Lancashire"
- "commercial access control Fylde Coast"
- "commercial consumer unit installation Blackpool"
- "commercial electrical testing Lancashire"
- "EPC survey Lancashire" (if Wilsons offers energy assessments for commercial clients)

**Proposed addition:** A `/commercial/` hub page summarising commercial capabilities, with links to relevant service pages. Not a priority while the residential pipeline is strong and site resources are limited — but worth building once the immediate gaps (EV, gate, guides) are covered.

## Keyword Research Methodology Note

The keyword volumes and competition assessments in this document are based on:
- Site structure analysis (identifying which service areas have no dedicated pages)
- Knowledge of local market competition on the Fylde Coast
- General knowledge of search intent patterns for UK trades and home services

For precise monthly search volume figures and competition scores, connect Google Search Console (once set up — see `06-seo-technical.md`) and cross-reference with a keyword tool such as Ahrefs, SEMrush, or the free Google Keyword Planner. The qualitative direction in this document is correct regardless of exact volumes; the intent patterns and competitive gaps are real.
