# SEO Keyword Research — Wilsons Systems

**Client:** Wilsons Systems / Wilsons Electrical Ltd
**Skill applied:** SEO Keyword Research
**Date:** May 2026

---

## Overview

Six keyword clusters, mapped to the page structure in File 2. Each cluster has a defined search intent — what the person typing this query actually wants. Intent determines the page type, the copy register, and the CTA.

**Intent types used here:**

- **Transactional** — ready to hire, need a phone number or quote form now
- **Commercial** — comparing options, will hire soon, want evidence of credibility
- **Informational** — researching, not yet ready to buy — needs a blog post, not a sales page

---

## Competitor Gap Analysis

**Primary competitor:** electricianblackpool.co.uk

- Has 30+ service pages and 9 location pages
- Ranks well for generic Blackpool electrician terms
- Does not appear to target smart home, Control4, or premium services
- Has no Control4 dealership
- Focuses on domestic electrical — not multi-discipline

**Wilsons' structural advantage:**

The multi-discipline capability (electrical + security + CCTV + smart home + fire alarms + networking + heating) means Wilsons can occupy keywords that no single-discipline competitor can touch. An alarm-only company cannot rank for "home cinema installation." An electrical-only company cannot rank for "Control4 installer." Wilsons can rank for all of them.

The brand authority comes from building the service hub structure (File 2) correctly. Once all nine service hubs and their sub-pages exist, Wilsons will have topical authority across more trades than any single competitor in the area — and the internal linking between them compounds over time.
